About the Library

A place to read,
learn and grow.

Sri Krishna Lending Library was founded with a simple purpose — to make quality books accessible to every member of our community. We believe reading is one of the most meaningful things a person can do.

Our collection spans fiction, non-fiction, spirituality, history, science, children's books and more. Whatever you are looking for, you are likely to find it here.

Simple Process

How lending works

  1. 01

    Explore the collection

    Browse books by title, author, category or language using our online catalogue.

  2. 02

    Choose your book

    Find a book you want to read and note its details before your visit.

  3. 03

    Visit the library and borrow

    Come to the library during opening hours and borrow the book at the counter.

  4. 04

    Return it by the due date

    Return the book in good condition by the agreed return date.

Library Rules

Borrow responsibly.

  • Return books on time and in good condition.
  • Handle books with care — avoid folds, marks or damage.
  • Keep the library a quiet, respectful space for everyone.
