About Sri Krishna
Lending Library

A community-centred library dedicated to fostering a love of reading, lifelong learning, and access to knowledge for everyone.

Sri Krishna Lending Library was established with a clear and heartfelt mission — to create a space where books are not a luxury, but an accessible resource for every member of our community. We believe that reading transforms lives, broadens perspectives, and builds stronger communities.

Our collection spans thousands of titles across fiction, non-fiction, self-help, spirituality, science, history, and children's literature. Whether you are a student seeking knowledge, a professional looking to grow, or someone searching for a quiet escape — our library has something for you.

We are proud to serve a growing community of readers who trust us to curate a meaningful, diverse, and high-quality collection. Every book in our library has been carefully selected to add value to our readers' lives.

Access to Knowledge

We believe quality books should be accessible to all. Our affordable lending model ensures that no reader is held back by cost.

Building Community

Our library is more than a collection of books — it is a gathering place for curious minds and a hub for learning and connection.

Lifelong Learning

From children discovering their first stories to adults exploring complex ideas, we champion reading as a lifelong journey.
